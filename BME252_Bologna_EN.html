Goals

This course aims to introduce students to the modern methods of mechanics applied to biological systems.

Content

Methods of vector algebra and calculus in their vector forms, as well as the indicial form. The Kronecker and permutation operators. Concepts relating to the deformation and motion of continuum media. Introduction of the concept of mass, force, surface traction, and stress. Derivation of the fundamental principles of conservation.

Contribution of the Course to Engineering Education

Open a pathway for the student to pursue further studies in tissue biomechanics, by laying a solid foundation of mechanical concepts and principles.
