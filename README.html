Why does this course exist?

The first course in Biomechanics offered by the Biomedical Engineering Department aims to enhance the understanding of the science of motion and mechanical interactions between living tissue.

Essential reading: Chapter 1 of [FUNG/94, HUMP/15, FUNG/04] (see References below)

  • Point of Departure, the development of biomechanics
  • Health Care Applications, complex mechanical phenomenon in living organisms
  • What Is Continuum Mechanics?
  • A Brief on Cell Biology,
  • The Extracellular Matrix
  • Mechanotransduction in Cells

Course learning objectives-CLOs

  • [ ] read and convert expressions between their tensor, matrix and indicial forms

  • [ ] state the balance of mass
  • [ ] differentiate between surface and body forces

Modus Operandi

Course is composed of a few activities,

  • Lectures
    • Listening and taking notes in the lecture
    • Prepare for next lectures with study questions and openAI
  • Attendence through A7 QR code
  • Quizzes
  • Exit Survey Questions
  • Final

Lectures

  • The board, projector, and supplementary materials will be utilized.
  • Asking questions, and taking notes are encouraged.
  • The lecture notes are shared on Yulearn.

Quizzes

Four quizzes, half-an-hour with a single question each, will be given during the semester.

Exit Survey Questions

Nine or ten questions shall be asked to students during the last 5 minutes of lectures during the semester.

Code of Conduct & Ethics

  • The interpersonal communication in class will require respect, punctuality and patience towards each other.
  • The class is a communal space, it is not a stage to show off (except for the instructor).
  • Students are expected to arrive to class on a voluntary basis, involuntary participation is strongly discouraged.
  • Mechanics requires a direct teaching model where the role of the student is usually limited to a receiver, however, the students are encouraged to ask questions and participate in the learning process.
  • Here’s a fascinating account of ethics (in Turkish): Etik Değerler ve Eğitimi by Prof. Ioanna Kuçuradi (Maltepe University).
